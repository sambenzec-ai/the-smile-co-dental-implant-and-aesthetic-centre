Meet Your Dentist

A Gentler Standard of Dental Care

Dr. Preety founded The Smile Co. with one conviction — that world-class dentistry should feel calm, transparent and completely painless. She is certified in advanced pain-free protocols and precision implantology, combining rotary endodontics with digital planning so treatments are faster, safer and remarkably comfortable.

For complex cases, Dr. Preety leads multidisciplinary coordination with consulting oral surgeons and orthodontists — orchestrating implants, alignments and full-mouth rehabilitations under one seamless, personally-supervised plan.
